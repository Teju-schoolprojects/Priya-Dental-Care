Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to common questions about dental care, treatments, and operations at Priya Dental Care.

Dentists deal with a wide variety of oral health problems including cleanings, fillings, root canals, and crowns. Orthodontists are specialized dentists who focus specifically on correcting the alignment of teeth and jaws using braces, aligners, and dental appliances.

Yes, a few dentists are qualified and certified as both orthodontists and dentists, allowing them to manage cleanings and aligners under the same roof. You can check directly with Priya Dental Care during your consultation regarding specific treatment programs.

It is standard clinical practice to visit a dentist twice a year, or once every three to six months, to clean plaque and identify decay early before it warrants complex root canal treatments.

Priya Dental Care specializes in a wide spectrum of dental procedures, including diagnostic consultations, preventive dental hygiene, pediatric care, painless root canals (RCT), tooth extraction, braces, dental crowns, bridges, and customized dental restorations.

Priya Dental Care operates all seven days of the week. Our doors open from Monday to Sunday, between 9:30 AM and 8:30 PM, ensuring convenience for working individuals and emergencies alike.
